Preferred Genres
These are your canonical genre labels. When fetching genres from the API, any subject containing one of these terms will be normalised to that label — e.g. "Horror Fiction" or "Gothic Horror" both become Horror if you have Horror in this list. Comma-separate doesn't apply here — each tag is a single genre.
Bulk Genre Backfill
Automatically fetch genres for all books that don't have one yet. Uses Open Library (best coverage) with Google Books as a fallback, then normalises results against your Preferred Genres list above.
